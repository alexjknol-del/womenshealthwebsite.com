Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains what data Women's Health Circle processes and why.

What we collect

Women's Health Circle has no account or checkout functionality. We only process information you share with us directly by email, such as your name and email address, to respond to your inquiry.

Retention

Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing info@womenshealthwebsite.com.
