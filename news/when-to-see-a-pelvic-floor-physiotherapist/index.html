When to See a Pelvic Floor Physiotherapist

Pelvic floor physiotherapy is underused relative to how common pelvic floor dysfunction actually is, partly because symptoms are often normalized rather than flagged as treatable.

Symptoms warranting an assessment include: urinary leakage with coughing, laughing, or exercise; pain during penetration; a sensation of pelvic heaviness or pressure; and persistent lower back or hip pain without another clear cause.

A physiotherapist can determine through internal assessment whether the underlying issue is weakness, over-tension, or a combination — a distinction that's difficult to make accurately through self-assessment alone.

Following a professional assessment, home-based tools such as biofeedback trainers can support consistent practice between appointments, particularly when weakness has been confirmed as the primary issue.
