Health & Wellness Writer, Women’s Health Circle

Naomi Cole

“Good information is the shortest path to a decision you won’t second-guess.”

Naomi has spent her career writing about women's health for consumer health publications, translating research and clinical guidance into language that's actually useful at 11pm when you're trying to figure out what's normal.

She built Women's Health Circle around a simple frustration: sexual health information online tends to split into two extremes — overly clinical resources that assume a medical background, or vague lifestyle content with no substance. She wanted the middle ground: grounded, specific, and readable.

Every topic on this platform follows the same structure: background, what the evidence actually says, and practical takeaways. Women's Health Circle does not sell products and is not a substitute for medical advice from your own doctor.
